Thailand

Pattaya · Bangkok · Phuket · Krabi

Our most-booked first trip abroad, and the easiest one to get right. Island speedboats, the Alcazar show, Nong Nooch gardens, and a Bangkok city day that keeps the shoppers happy.

Best November to February

Worth building the trip around

  1. The Grand Palace and Wat Pho

    Go at opening time. By eleven the courtyards are full and the heat is punishing.

  2. Coral Island by speedboat

    A half day from Pattaya. Lunch on the beach, and calm enough for children.

  3. The Alcazar cabaret

    Pattaya's long-running show. Book the earlier slot if you have young kids.

  4. Nong Nooch gardens

    Bigger than people expect. Budget three hours, not one.

  5. Safari World and Marine Park

    The single best day for families travelling with children under twelve.

  6. Krabi or Phi Phi

    Worth adding three nights if you want beach time rather than city time.

Dubai & Abu Dhabi

Dubai · Abu Dhabi · Desert

Short flight, no jet lag, and something for every age in the group. Desert safari with dinner, the observation deck, a dhow cruise, and a day across to Abu Dhabi.

Best November to March

Worth building the trip around

  1. Burj Khalifa observation deck

    Book a sunset slot weeks ahead. It sells out, and the queue at the door is not worth it.

  2. Desert safari with dinner

    Dune bashing, camels and a barbecue under lights. The one everyone remembers.

  3. Dhow cruise on the Marina

    A quiet evening after two hard sightseeing days. Dinner is included.

  4. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque

    A day trip to Abu Dhabi. Modest dress is required and enforced.

  5. Dubai Mall and the fountain

    Free, and better after dark. Pair it with the Burj visit.

  6. Global Village

    Seasonal, roughly October to April. Skip it in summer.

Singapore

Sentosa · Marina Bay · Jurong

The one families with children ask for by name. Universal Studios, the Night Safari, Gardens by the Bay, and a city so easy to move around that nobody gets tired of it.

Good all year

Worth building the trip around

  1. Universal Studios, Sentosa

    A full day. The reason most families pick Singapore in the first place.

  2. Gardens by the Bay

    The Supertree light show runs twice each evening and costs nothing to watch.

  3. Singapore Night Safari

    An open tram through the dark. Younger children usually prefer it to the day zoo.

  4. Sentosa cable car and beaches

    Half a day, and an easy one to slot in around the theme park.

  5. Jurong Bird Park and the river

    Good for a slower morning when the group needs a gentler pace.

  6. Little India and Chinatown

    Where you will find the vegetarian food, and the best value meals in the city.

Bali

Ubud · Seminyak · Nusa Penida

Where we send honeymooners, and anyone who wants the holiday to feel slow. Rice terraces, cliff temples at sunset, waterfalls, and a private villa if that is the mood.

Best April to October

Worth building the trip around

  1. Tanah Lot at sunset

    Arrive an hour early. The temple sits on a rock the tide cuts off.

  2. Tegallalang rice terraces

    Early morning, before the tour buses and before the heat.

  3. Nusa Penida day trip

    A rough boat crossing and a long day, but the coastline is the reason people come.

  4. Ubud and the art villages

    Wood carving, silver, painting. Also where the good cafes are.

  5. Uluwatu temple and the kecak dance

    Clifftop, at sunset, with a fire dance afterwards.

  6. Waterfalls near Munduk

    Sekumpul and Gitgit. Steep steps down, so not one for weak knees.

Vietnam

Hanoi · Ha Long · Da Nang

Still underrated, and remarkable value for what you see. An overnight cruise through Ha Long Bay, the old quarter of Hanoi, and the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills.

Best October to April

Worth building the trip around

  1. An overnight cruise on Ha Long Bay

    Sleep on the boat. The day-trip version misses the morning, which is the best part.

  2. Hoi An old town

    Lanterns after dark, and tailors who will turn a suit around in a day.

  3. The Golden Bridge, Ba Na Hills

    Reached by one of the longest cable cars in the world.

  4. Hanoi's old quarter

    Street food, the water puppet theatre, and traffic you will never forget.

  5. Marble Mountains, Da Nang

    Caves and pagodas cut into the rock. There is a lift for the first level.

  6. Cu Chi tunnels

    If you are routing through Ho Chi Minh City rather than the north.

Maldives

Male atolls · Resort islands

For the anniversary or the honeymoon where the point is to do nothing at all. Overwater villa, house reef off your deck, and a seaplane transfer that becomes its own memory.

Best November to April

Worth building the trip around

  1. A night in an overwater villa

    Even two nights of the trip. Steps from your deck into the lagoon.

  2. Snorkelling the house reef

    Included at most resorts. No boat, no booking, just walk in.

  3. A sandbank picnic

    Lunch on a strip of sand with nothing else on it.

  4. Sunset dolphin cruise

    Roughly two hours. Spinner dolphins are common rather than lucky.

  5. The seaplane transfer

    Not just transport. The approach over the atolls is worth a window seat.

  6. A spa treatment over the water

    Glass floor, and the reason a lot of couples come back.

Malaysia

Kuala Lumpur · Genting · Langkawi

Pairs beautifully with Singapore for a two-country trip. Twin towers, the cable car up to Genting, and island time in Langkawi for the back half.

Good all year

Worth building the trip around

  1. Petronas Towers skybridge

    Timed tickets, and they go early. The park below is free and good at night.

  2. Batu Caves

    272 painted steps up to the shrine. Cover your knees and shoulders.

  3. Genting Highlands

    Cable car up into the cloud, then a cool evening. A relief after KL heat.

  4. Langkawi's Sky Bridge

    A curved walkway suspended off the mountain, reached by cable car.

  5. Island hopping from Langkawi

    Half a day by boat, and easy enough with children aboard.

  6. Bukit Bintang after dark

    Where the food and the night markets are.

Sri Lanka

Sigiriya · Kandy · Ella · Bentota

Close enough for a short holiday, varied enough to feel far away. Rock fortresses, tea country by train, temple towns, and a few days on the beach to finish.

Best December to March

Worth building the trip around

  1. Climbing Sigiriya

    1,200 steps and no shade. Start at seven, and skip it if knees are a problem.

  2. The Kandy to Ella train

    Widely called one of the great rail journeys. Book reserved seats early.

  3. The Nine Arch Bridge

    A short walk from Ella town. Time it for a passing train.

  4. Temple of the Tooth, Kandy

    Go for the evening puja. White dress is customary.

  5. A tea estate above Nuwara Eliya

    Picking, factory, tasting. Cool enough to need a jacket.

  6. Bentota or Mirissa beach

    Two or three nights at the end to slow the trip down.

Quieter than the Maldives and easier on the budget, with far more to actually do. Catamaran days, the coloured earth at Chamarel, waterfalls, and a genuinely warm welcome.

Kashmir

Srinagar · Gulmarg · Pahalgam

No passport, no visa, and it still stops people in their tracks. A night on a Dal Lake houseboat, the Gulmarg gondola, and meadows at Pahalgam that photographs never do justice.

Best March to October

Worth building the trip around

  1. A night on a Dal Lake houseboat

    Carved cedar interiors, and a shikara to the floating market at dawn.

  2. The Gulmarg gondola

    Two stages. The upper one runs to 3,980 metres and is weather dependent.

  3. Pahalgam and Betaab valley

    Meadows, a river, and ponies for the stretch beyond the road head.

  4. Sonmarg and Thajiwas glacier

    A day trip from Srinagar, open roughly April to November.

  5. The Mughal gardens

    Nishat and Shalimar, terraced down to the lake. Gentle walking.

  6. Srinagar's old city

    Papier-mache, pashmina and walnut wood, bought where they are made.

Kerala

Alleppey · Munnar · Thekkady

The gentlest holiday on this list, and the one elders enjoy most. A private houseboat through the backwaters, tea estates at Munnar, and unhurried days throughout.

Best September to March

Worth building the trip around

  1. An overnight houseboat at Alleppey

    Private boat, cooked-aboard meals, and the backwaters going quiet at dusk.

  2. Tea estates at Munnar

    Plantation walks and a working factory. Bring something warm for the evening.

  3. Periyar at Thekkady

    A boat on the lake at first light is the best chance of seeing elephants.

  4. A Kathakali performance

    Arrive early to watch the makeup being applied. It is half the show.

  5. Ayurvedic treatments

    Most resorts have a centre. Worth building two or three sessions into the week.

  6. Fort Kochi

    Chinese fishing nets, Jew Town, and colonial streets that are good for walking.

Rajasthan

Udaipur · Jaipur · Jaisalmer

Lake palaces, hill forts, and a night under canvas in the Thar. Works as a family circuit or as a heritage-hotel trip for two, depending on how you want it built.

Best October to March

Worth building the trip around

  1. Amber Fort and Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

    Amber early, before the crowds. Hawa Mahal photographs best from across the road.

  2. City Palace and a boat on Lake Pichola

    Udaipur at sunset, which is the image most people have of Rajasthan.

  3. Jaisalmer fort

    A fort people still live inside, rather than a monument they visit.

  4. A night in a desert camp

    Camels, dunes and folk music. Cold after dark, even in season.

  5. Mehrangarh, Jodhpur

    The blue city seen from the ramparts. One of the best-kept forts in India.

  6. Ranakpur or Pushkar

    Marble Jain temples, or the lake town, depending on how the route runs.

Documentation desk

Indian passport assistance

A separate service from the holidays, and one people mostly hear about from a relative. We help applicants across Pune, Sangli and the wider Maharashtra region get through the process without losing a working day to it.

What we help with

  • Fresh applications and renewals
  • Tatkal appointments when the travel date is close
  • Minor passports for children under 18
  • Form filling, appointment slots and document checks
  • Corrections to name, address or date of birth

Keep these ready

  • Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or Driving Licence
  • Bank passbook, electricity bill or rent agreement
  • Birth certificate or 10th school leaving certificate
  • For minors, both parents' passports or IDs

Fees are set by the Passport Seva Kendra

They change by booklet type, validity, applicant age and whether you go Tatkal, so we do not publish a figure here. Call and describe your case and we will tell you exactly which category applies and what the government charges for it.

Before you call

The things people ask us first.

What will my holiday cost?

There is no price list on this page, and that is deliberate. The same Thailand trip can differ by a wide margin between October and December purely on airfare, and hotel category moves it again. Tell us your dates and who is travelling, and you get a written quotation with flights, stays, transfers and sightseeing itemised separately - so you can see exactly what you are paying for, and change it.

How far ahead should I book?

For international travel in peak season - Diwali, Christmas, or the May school holidays - two to three months gives you the best airfare and the hotels you actually want. Off-season, three to four weeks is usually enough. Kashmir and Kerala in season fill earlier than people expect.

Do you handle visas?

Yes, as part of planning the trip. We tell you which documents are needed, check them before submission, and track the application. Thailand, Dubai, Singapore, Malaysia, Sri Lanka and Maldives are all straightforward for Indian passport holders. The passport desk is a separate service for the passport itself.

Which airport will we fly from?

Mumbai or Pune for most international departures. If your group is large or the fare works out better, we will look at Delhi, Bengaluru or Hyderabad and tell you honestly whether the saving is worth the extra travel.

My parents are travelling. Can you plan around that?

This is a large part of what we do. Walking distances get checked, sightseeing days get shortened, rest gaps go into the itinerary, wheelchair assistance is arranged at every airport, and vegetarian or Jain meals are confirmed with hotels before arrival rather than requested at the counter.

What if something goes wrong while we are there?

You message us. A delayed flight, a room that is not what was promised, a transfer that did not turn up - it gets handled from this end while you are still standing there. That is the actual difference between booking through us and booking it yourself online.

Do you take corporate and group bookings?

Yes. Corporate offsites, MICE movements, family groups and wedding parties. Group airfare holds, single-point billing, and for larger movements a coordinator who travels with the group.
